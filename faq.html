The procedure

Who actually performs the hair transplant?

The International Society of Hair Restoration Surgery (ISHRS) considers three steps to be surgery that a licensed doctor should perform: designing the hairline, harvesting the grafts and making the recipient incisions (channels). In Turkey, the Hair Transplant Units Regulation (amended September 2025) allows only certified physicians to open the channels; certified health professionals may extract and implant grafts under a doctor's supervision. Before you book any clinic, ask who will do each step, by name.

What is a graft, and how many will I need?

A graft is a follicular unit: a small natural group of usually 1 to 4 hairs. So 3,000 grafts is not 3,000 hairs, it is typically several thousand more. How many you need depends on the area to cover, your hair loss pattern and how much your donor area can safely give. Your donor supply is limited, so a good plan also keeps some in reserve for the future.

FUE or DHI: which is better?

Both start the same way: grafts are taken one by one from the donor area (FUE extraction). The difference is placement. In classic FUE the channels are opened first and the grafts placed into them; in DHI the grafts are loaded into an implanter pen and placed directly. Neither is better for everyone. The surgeon's skill and planning matter more than the name of the technique.

Does a hair transplant hurt?

The procedure is done under local anaesthetic, so the main discomfort is the anaesthetic injections at the start. Most patients describe mild soreness and tightness for a few days afterwards, managed with the medication the clinic provides.

Your trip and recovery

How long do I need to stay in Istanbul?

Usually 3 to 4 days: arrival and consultation, the procedure day, and a check-up with the first wash before you fly home. The clinic confirms the exact schedule for your case.

How much time off work will I need?

Many people return to desk work after about a week. Scabs and redness usually settle within 7 to 10 days. Avoid heavy exercise, swimming and direct sun on the scalp for the period your clinic advises.

Is it normal for the transplanted hair to fall out?

Yes. Most transplanted hairs shed within the first few weeks. The roots stay in place. New hair usually starts growing around months 3 to 4, and the final result is typically visible at 12 to 18 months.

Is it safe to get a hair transplant in Istanbul?

Istanbul has many licensed, experienced clinics, but like any surgery a hair transplant carries risks, and quality varies between clinics. Choose a licensed clinic where a doctor plans your case and performs the surgical steps, check real results from the same doctor, and get everything included in writing. No clinic can honestly guarantee 100% growth.

Choosing a clinic

What should I ask a clinic before booking?

Who designs my hairline, takes the grafts and opens the channels? How many patients does the doctor treat that day? Will I meet the doctor before the procedure? Who estimated my graft number? How much donor will I have left for the future? What exactly is included? What happens if I need a touch-up? Can I see this doctor's real 12-month results? If a clinic avoids these questions, that is your answer.
