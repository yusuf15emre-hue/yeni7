How does Lucera Global get paid?

Our partner clinics pay us a referral fee when a patient we introduce goes ahead with treatment. We tell you the full price, and exactly what it includes, in writing before you book. You never pay us for the consultation or the graft estimate.

Who actually performs the hair transplant?

The International Society of Hair Restoration Surgery (ISHRS) considers three steps to be surgery that a licensed doctor should perform: designing the hairline, harvesting the grafts and making the recipient incisions (channels). In Turkey, the Hair Transplant Units Regulation (amended September 2025) allows only certified physicians to open the channels; certified health professionals may extract and implant grafts under a doctor's supervision. Before you book any clinic, ask who will do each step, by name.

How long do I need to stay in Istanbul?

Usually 3 to 4 days: arrival and consultation, the procedure day, and a check-up with the first wash before you fly home. The clinic confirms the exact schedule for your case.

Is it normal for the transplanted hair to fall out?

Yes. Most transplanted hairs shed within the first few weeks. The roots stay in place. New hair usually starts growing around months 3 to 4, and the final result is typically visible at 12 to 18 months.

Is it safe to get a hair transplant in Istanbul?

Istanbul has many licensed, experienced clinics, but like any surgery a hair transplant carries risks, and quality varies between clinics. Choose a licensed clinic where a doctor plans your case and performs the surgical steps, check real results from the same doctor, and get everything included in writing. No clinic can honestly guarantee 100% growth.
