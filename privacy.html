Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

This website

This site has no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Fonts and images are served from our own domain. Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard technical server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operations.

What you send us

When you contact us on WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok or by email, we receive your name, phone number or username, your messages and any photos you send, such as photos of your scalp. Photos of your scalp can be health information, so we treat them with extra care.

We use this information only to answer you, prepare a graft estimate and quote, and help arrange your treatment. With your permission, we share your photos and the details needed for your treatment with the partner clinic, so its medical team can assess your case.

We never publish your photos or story without your separate written consent, and we never sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your conversation and photos only as long as needed to help you and follow up on your treatment, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by writing to info@luceraglobal.com or messaging us on WhatsApp. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok and Gmail are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies, which apply when you use them to contact us.

Lucera Global is a patient-referral agency, not a health facility. Treatment is provided by licensed partner clinics in Istanbul. Information on this site is general and not medical advice.
